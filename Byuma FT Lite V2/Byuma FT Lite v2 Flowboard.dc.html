BYUMA FT LITE V2
Every screen
Seventeen states, in the order a person meets them. Numbers pair with the developer spec.
1 · Getting in
1.1 Sign up
Name, email, password (8+), or Google. Lands on an empty home with zero balance.
1.2 Sign in
Returning user. Failures name the field and colour its border red.
1.3 Nothing recorded yet
Recorder is live; the timeline and totals are replaced by one line of reassurance.
1.4 Something broke
Full-screen stop with two ways out. Also the post-sign-out state.
2 · Recording
2.1 Record an expense
Amount, Cash / MoMo / Bank, what it was for, numpad, then the timeline below.
2.2 Categories
The chips above the numpad. Use count per category; removal keeps old labels.
2.3 Everything deleted
Distinct from 1.3 so a person can tell a wipe from a fresh start.
3 · Balance and analytics
3.1 Analytics
Currency tabs, Spendable, Must and Safety net, then month total, split, categories, six months.
3.2 Update balance
One total per picked currency, replacing the old ones, plus money from a fourth currency at an editable rate.
3.3 Limits
Must (strict, red) and Safety net (flexible, violet) per currency, with a live spendable preview.
3.4 Currencies
Add a code, tick up to three, delete custom ones. Rates to the main currency listed per row.
4 · Profile
4.1 Profile
Account rows, main currency, three settings, data count, delete all, sign out.
4.2 Change name
Saves with a freeze, returns to profile, confirms by toast.
4.3 Change email
Current email shown, password confirms, then a confirm sheet.
4.4 Change password
Three fields and a strength meter: weak, fair, strong.
5 · Warnings and freezes
5.1 Confirm before it happens
Deleting an expense or all of them, signing out, changing email or password.
5.2 Frozen while it runs
Blocking veil with a spinner and a label. Nothing is tappable until it finishes.
